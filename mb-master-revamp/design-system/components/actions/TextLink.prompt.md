Every inline link on the site. Underline draws in from the right on hover, out to the left on leave, so descenders stay readable.

```jsx
<TextLink href="/results/">See what actually happened</TextLink>
<TextLink href="https://x.com/mikebastin" external size=".88rem">X</TextLink>
```

Use it for nav items and footer links too. Do not add text-decoration.
