Reviews shown in the language they were written in, on the locale that can read them.

```jsx
<TestimonialCard
  quote="…"
  name="Joost van Dijk"
  when="3 months ago"
  langLabel="Nederlands"
  lang="nl"
  english="…"
  localGuide
/>
```

Two rules that are not cosmetic: the surname is reduced to an initial in the display, and no review schema is emitted anywhere near this component.
