A French page shows English copy, or enquiries from one market drop overnight. Each is quick to confirm on the page itself, and each costs enquiries for every week it waits for the next audit.

Below are the ten Chrome extensions we keep installed for those moments, grouped by job, and the point where a quick check stops being enough.

## The ten extensions at a glance

Every one has a free tier, so trying the set costs an afternoon, not a budget line.

| Extension | Best for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEO Minion | On-page checks, hreflang, broken links | Free |
| Ahrefs SEO Toolbar | On-page reports and SERP overlays | Free, more with a subscription |
| Detailed SEO Extension | One-click page summary | Free |
| Web Developer | Testing pages without JavaScript or CSS | Free |
| Redirect Path | Redirect chains and status codes | Free |
| Keywords Everywhere | Keyword data inside Google results | Free ideas, paid credits for volume |
| MozBar | Authority metrics and link highlighting | Free account, more with Moz Pro |
| Similarweb | Traffic estimates for any site | Free |
| Wappalyzer | Spotting a site's technology stack | Free |
| Tag Assistant | Checking Google tags fire correctly | Free |

## On-page and technical checks

Most faults that cost a page its traffic are visible on the page itself. These five find them in seconds, without a full crawl.

### SEO Minion

[SEO Minion](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/seo-minion/giihipjfimkajhlcilipnjeohabimjhi?hl=en) covers most on-page analysis for free. It shows meta tags, headings and other on-page elements, broken links, and nofollow, sponsored and UGC links, previews the search result, and checks hreflang, where multilingual sites most often misfire.

### Ahrefs SEO Toolbar

The [Ahrefs SEO Toolbar](https://ahrefs.com/seo-toolbar) flags missing or incorrect elements, robots.txt and meta robots settings, and Open Graph tags. The free version is useful on its own.

### Detailed SEO Extension

The [Detailed SEO Extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/detailed-seo-extension/pfjdepjjfjjahkjfpkcgfmfhmnakjfba?hl=en) summarises any page in one click: title and meta description, heading structure, internal and external link counts, schema markup, and shortcuts to robots.txt and the sitemap. It is the fastest way to decide whether a page needs a closer look.

### Web Developer

The [Web Developer extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/web-developer/bfbameneiokkgbdmiekhjnmfkcnldhhm?hl=en-US) answers one question that matters to rankings: what does search see if the JavaScript never runs? It also outlines headings, links and images and validates HTML and CSS.

### Redirect Path

[Redirect Path](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/redirect-path/aomidfkchockcldhbkggjokdkkebmdll?hl=en) shows every hop a URL takes, with the HTTP status code at each step. Chains and loops slow the page and leak link value, and here you see them at a glance. Our [technical SEO audit checklist](/blog/technical-seo-audit-checklist/) explains what to do with what it finds.

## Keywords and competitors: extensions for sizing up a market

What a competitor ranks for, and what it earns them, tells you whether a market is worth the budget before you commit it.

### Keywords Everywhere

[Keywords Everywhere](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/keywords-everywhere-keywo/hbapdpeemoojbophdfndmlgdhppljgmp) puts keyword data beside Google results. Related keywords, long-tail ideas and difficulty scores are free; volume, cost per click and trends use paid credits.

### MozBar

[MozBar](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/mozbar/eakacpaijcpapndcfffdgphdiccmpknp?hl=en) shows Page Authority and Domain Authority (with a free Moz account), on-page elements and follow versus nofollow links. Spam Score and deeper analysis need Moz Pro.

### Similarweb

Not strictly an SEO tool, Similarweb shows you how a competitor gets its visitors: estimated traffic, traffic sources, top referring sites and audience interests for any domain. Treat the numbers as estimates and compare trends rather than totals.

### Wappalyzer

[Wappalyzer](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/wappalyzer-technology-pro/gppongmhjkpfnbhagpmjfkannfbllamg) identifies the technology behind a site: CMS, ecommerce platform, analytics tools, JavaScript frameworks and server software. Handy for competitor research and audits.

## Tracking

A tag that stops firing makes a market look weaker than it is, and the next budget decision follows the wrong number.

### Tag Assistant

Google's [Tag Assistant](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tag-assistant/kejbdjndbnbjgmefkgdddjlbokphdefk?hl=en) checks that Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Google Ads conversion tags fire correctly, with tagassistant.google.com for in-page debugging. It replaces the older Legacy and Companion extensions, so install this one. Tags that fail quietly are the usual reason SEO results look worse in reports than they are.

## Using them well

Extensions answer questions about one page. For anything site-wide, a crawler and Search Console are still the source of truth. Keep the set small, since some read every page you visit, and respect each site's terms of service when pulling data.
