## Simplifying complex legal and bureaucratic steps

The foundation of successful **company registration in Valencia** is choosing the right legal structure based on liability, tax efficiency, and growth goals.

Most founders choose between two models:

| | Autónomo (sole trader) | Sociedad Limitada (S.L.) |
|---|---|---|
| Suits | Freelancers, consultants, solo services | Teams, investors, growth plans |
| Liability | Unlimited, personal | Limited to the company |
| Minimum capital | None | €1 (legal reserve rules below €3,000) |
| Setup | Fast, low cost | Notary deed and registry entry |

The S.L. minimum share capital has been €1 since the Crea y Crece law of 2022. Below €3,000, though, the company must put at least 20% of its profit into the legal reserve until reserve and capital together reach €3,000, and in a liquidation the partners answer for the shortfall up to that figure. Many founders therefore still capitalise at €3,000.

> The capital of a limited company may not be less than one euro. At least 20 percent of profit must go to the legal reserve until that reserve, together with the share capital, reaches €3,000.
>
> Source: [BOE, Ley 18/2022, de 28 de septiembre, de creación y crecimiento de empresas](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-15818)

## Required documents and local regulations

Successful **business registration in Valencia** hinges on meticulous documentation. In our experience, missing or inconsistent paperwork is the most common cause of delays, turning weeks into months.

### Essential identification documents

-   **NIE or DNI** (for residents)
-   **Valid passport** (non-EU citizens)
-   **Proof of address** in Valencia (e.g., utility bill, rental contract)

### Company formation documentation (S.L.)

-   **Certificate of name reservation** from the Registro Mercantil Central
-   **Articles of Association** (drafted in Spanish, notarized)
-   **Public deed of incorporation** signed before a Spanish notary
-   **Bank certificate** confirming your share capital deposit (€1 minimum by law; see the legal reserve rule above before choosing a lower figure than €3,000)

### Tax and compliance essentials

-   **Model 036 or 037** for census registration
-   **CNAE code(s)** defining your economic activity (e.g., 6210 for computer programming under CNAE-2025, which replaced 6201 in the 2009 classification)
-   **VAT (IVA) registration**, plus entry in the intra-EU operators register (ROI/VIES) if you trade B2B with other EU countries

> CNAE-2025 code 6210, "Actividades de programación informática", corresponds to code 6201 in CNAE-2009.
>
> Source: [Idescat, statistical classifications, CNAE-2025 code 6210](https://www.idescat.cat/classificacions/?tc=6&v0=1&id=cnae-2025-es&v3=6210&lang=es)

### Social Security and municipal requirements

-   **Autónomo registration** with the Tesorería General de la Seguridad Social, usually online through Import@ss
-   **Workplace risk assessment** (for physical premises)
-   **Licencia de apertura** from Ayuntamiento de València (varies by activity: basic vs. qualified licence)
-   **Signage permit** and health/safety certifications (if applicable)

## Timeline and government offices involved

**Business registration in Valencia** involves multiple agencies, each with distinct procedures:

| Office | What it handles |
|---|---|
| Notary public | Incorporation deed |
| Registro Mercantil de Valencia | Registering the company |
| Agencia Tributaria | Tax and VAT registration |
| Tesorería General de la Seguridad Social | Social security |
| Ayuntamiento de València | Municipal licences (Plaza del Ayuntamiento, 1) |

Realistic timeframes, from our experience in 2026:

-   **Name reservation**: 2 to 5 business days (online)
-   **Notary appointment**: 1 to 2 weeks
-   **Commercial Registry**: 10 to 15 days (standard), up to 4 weeks (complex cases)
-   **Municipal licence**: 2 weeks (simple activity) to 3 months (premises requiring inspections)

Pro tip: processes often run on “relationship time”, not clock time. Build a generous buffer into every estimate, and avoid August, when many offices operate with skeleton crews.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Do we need to live in Valencia to register a business there?**  
A: No. You can register a business in Valencia without residency, but you’ll need a Spanish address (e.g., virtual office or lawyer’s address) for official correspondence and a valid NIE.

**Q: How long does it take to register a Sociedad Limitada (S.L.) in Valencia?**  
A: Typically 3 to 6 weeks from name reservation to full operational status, assuming clean documentation and no delays in notary or municipal licensing.

**Q: Can we register as an autónomo with a tourist visa?**  
A: No. You must have legal residency or a work permit to register as an autónomo. Non-EU citizens usually apply for a self-employed residence and work visa (_visado de residencia y trabajo por cuenta propia_), or for the entrepreneur visa if the project meets its criteria, before registration.

**Q: What is a “licencia de apertura,” and do we need one?**  
A: Yes, if you operate from a physical location (shop, office, restaurant). The Ayuntamiento de València issues this municipal licence. Simple activities (e.g., consulting) may qualify for a “declaración responsable” (faster, cheaper); others require technical inspections.
