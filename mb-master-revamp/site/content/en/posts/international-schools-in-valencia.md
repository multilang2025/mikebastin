## Best International Schools in Valencia, Spain

Choosing an international school in Valencia takes real research, but narrowing it down to the factors that matter makes the process manageable.

Accreditation, curriculum, language options, and overall academic quality are the things worth focusing on.

Together, they determine whether a school will actually serve your child’s needs. The [Valencia international schools directory](https://valenciamove.com/international-schools-valencia/) compares the main options side by side.

### Accreditation and Quality Assurance

Accreditation confirms that a school meets defined quality standards, verified by an external body rather than self-reported.

A number of international schools in Valencia hold recognised credentials of this kind.

[Linguaschools](https://www.linguaschools.com/valencia/) in Valencia is one example.

It is accredited by the Instituto Cervantes, a meaningful marker for anyone prioritising serious Spanish-language instruction: [Instituto Cervantes](https://cervantes.org/).

Other schools hold accreditation from the Federación Española de Escuelas de Español para Extranjeros (FEDELE), a body focused on quality standards in Spanish-language teaching ([FEDELE](https://fedele.org/)).

These credentials give parents a reliable way to confirm a school’s teaching standards before committing.

## Choosing the Right International School

Choosing a school for your children in Valencia involves weighing several factors at once, each with its own trade-offs.

The key questions worth asking: what is the school’s academic culture, what will it cost, and what does the admissions process actually involve?

### Academic Focus and Atmosphere

Schools in Valencia vary considerably in teaching philosophy and character.

Some favour a student-led approach, others a more traditional, structured model. Points worth considering:

-   **Teaching Style**: Is the school student-led and inquiry-based, or more traditional and structured?
-   **Language**: Will your child be learning primarily in Spanish, English, or a bilingual mix?
-   **Enrichment**: Does the school offer arts, sport, and other activities alongside core academics?

Matching a school’s approach to your child’s learning style sets them up for a stronger experience overall. For a full breakdown, see the school directory above.

### The Admissions Process

Admission to a private school in Valencia involves a defined process rather than an informal enquiry ([Moving 2 Valencia](https://moving2valencia.eu/guide-to-private-and-international-schools-in-valencia/)). The typical steps:

1.  **Application forms**: Complete the application, usually available on the school’s website.
2.  **Documentation**: Schools typically request previous school reports, proof of identity, and other documents specific to their requirements.
3.  **Assessment**: Entrance tests or interviews are often part of the process.
4.  **Confirmation**: On acceptance, the registration fee secures your child’s place.

### International vs. Local Schools

What’s the real difference between international and local schools?

The two are not directly comparable.

International schools in Valencia teach in multiple languages and follow globally recognised curricula.

Programmes like the International Baccalaureate (IB) or the British curriculum are a strong fit if university abroad is the long-term plan.

Instruction is generally in English, which helps if a child’s Spanish is not yet strong.

**The comparison:**

-   **Local Schools:** Follow the Spanish curriculum, taught primarily in Spanish and, in this region, Valenciano. It’s an effective way to build local language and cultural fluency, and typically far more affordable than the international alternative.
