# Statistics log

Every statistic added to a blog article as a sourced blockquote, by the
`/blockquotes` skill (`.claude/skills/blockquotes/SKILL.md`). One row per figure
per translation group; the FR and ES versions quote the same source.

Re-check a row by opening its URL: the figure, its cohort and its period must
still be on the page. A figure that can no longer be found there is removed
from every locale, not re-sourced elsewhere.
