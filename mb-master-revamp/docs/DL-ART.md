# Recycled illustrations (owner, 30 Sep 2026)

The owner's design proposal for Delaguía y Luzón (dlvibe.delaguialuzon.fr) was
rejected by the client, so its 25 animated illustrations were recycled here.
They are the owner's own work product, used as drawn: the SVG markup and the
CSS animations are the originals.

- **Archive:** `design/dl-art/<name>.svg.html` and `<name>.css`, all 25, as
  extracted (CSS modules class names kept, so none collide). The original
  light-theme overrides were dropped.
- **Build:** `node scripts/build-dl-art.mjs` (in `site/`) writes
  `lib/dl-art-data.ts` and `app/dl-art.css` for the ones listed in `USED`.
- **Colour:** the originals read `--d3-*` and `--art-*` variables. `.dl-art`
  maps them to this site's band tokens: plate from `--bg` and `--ink`, lines
  and dots `--dim`, borders `--rule`, and the single accent `--berry`, so the
  art follows Night Swell and Morning Glass on every band.
- **Placement:** `lib/dl-art.ts`. Service heroes go through
  `components/ServiceHeroArt.tsx`, which prefers a recycled illustration over
  the generic motif; contact, how we work, pricing, results and the team
  pages use `components/DlArt.tsx` directly. Desktop only, like the existing
  hero art.

## World and Europe maps (owner, 30 Sep 2026)

"In EN and FR I don't want to limit myself to Spain, FR should feature
Europe, EN the world." The two Spain-map drawings also exist on a world map
(EN) and a Europe map (FR), generated by `design/dl-art/geo/make-variants.mjs`
from Natural Earth land (the `world-atlas` package), with Valencia as the hub
and real cities as origins (Houston, Santo Domingo, London, Dubai, Singapore
for the world; Paris, Brussels, Amsterdam, Berlin, Zurich, London for
Europe). Only the geometry changes: classes and animations are the same, so
the variants share the original's CSS. The Spanish SEO page keeps the Spain
map, because the page is about Spain.

## On mobile

Recycled illustrations show on phones too: `components/HeroArtSlot.tsx`
stacks them under the hero text, centred and capped at 320px (the originals'
own mobile rule). The generic hero motifs stay desktop only.
